Our Work

Advocating equality for all Ohioans

Equality Ohio defends the rights of LGBTQ Ohioans. We are Ohio’s only organization dedicated solely to the civil rights of LGBTQ Ohioans, and we work with partners across the wide landscape of the Ohio social justice movement.

1

In your community

We work in communities across the state to ensure that local laws reflect and protect LGBTQ residents.

2

At the Ohio Statehouse

We work to make sure that our state laws are fair to LGBTQ people — and that Ohio does not take a step backwards.

3

At the federal level

Equality Ohio is a voice that represents the interest of LGBTQ Ohioans in national policy debates.

We hold elected leaders accountable for decisions they make that impact the LGBTQ community. In addition, we work on policy issues with healthcare facilities, corporations, service providers, and more.

Through education, advocacy and mobilization, Equality Ohio works on the issues affecting LGBTQ people and our communities to achieve our vision:

An Ohio where everyone feels at home and where equality, diversity and inclusiveness are universally valued.
