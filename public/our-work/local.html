Our Work · Local Progress

Statewide community organizing

Equality Ohio works in communities across the state on a variety of issues that affect people — including inclusive nondiscrimination protections, banning conversion “therapy” on minors, increasing access to services for transgender people, and more.

Nondiscrimination

LGBTQ‑inclusive nondiscrimination laws

Until our state legislature passes the Ohio Fairness Act, it is up to cities and localities across the state to pass nondiscrimination ordinances in their local city and village councils to protect their community members.

Because of the patchwork of protections, many Ohioans lose their civil rights on their daily commute.
