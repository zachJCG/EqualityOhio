20th Anniversary Honorees

Celebrating our past, reclaiming our future

At “Advocates and Allies: Celebrating Our Past, Reclaiming Our Future” — Equality Ohio’s 20th anniversary celebration on October 17, 2025 — six trailblazers were honored for two decades of tireless progress, resilience, and collective pride in the fight for LGBTQ+ equality.

EU

Erin Upchurch

Jim Obergefell Legacy Advocate Award

Executive Director of Kaleidoscope Youth Center.

CE

Chad Eggspuehler

LeeAnn Massucci Legal Advocate of the Year

Partner at Tucker Ellis LLP.

BS

The Brown/Schultz Family

Legacy Ally Award

Among the first honorees announced for Equality Ohio’s 20th anniversary celebration.

AC

Arienne Childrey

Emerging Leader Award

City of St. Mary’s 4th Ward Council Member — the first transgender woman to become a city council member in Ohio, whose leadership affirms that representation matters and that every voice deserves to be heard.

MV

Monika Veliz

Queer Joy Award

CEO & President of Margie’s Hope and Executive Director of Margie’s Closet — more than a free consignment shop, a sanctuary offering gender-affirming clothing, resources, and community support in Cleveland and beyond.

SL

Dr. Scott Leibowitz

Queer Wellness Advocate of the Year

Former Medical Director of the THRIVE Program at Nationwide Children’s Hospital, which grew into the largest assessment and therapy program embedded in a multidisciplinary pediatric gender clinic in the country.

“Even when I didn’t have the knowledge or courage to fight for myself, Equality Ohio was fighting for all of us.”

— Arienne Childrey, Emerging Leader Award honoree

Legacy of Equality Society

Leave a legacy of equality

Our Legacy of Equality Society members are a group of generous and forward-thinking Equality Ohio supporters who have made a long-range commitment to the success of our mission to bring full equality and justice to Ohio’s LGBTQ community. Their gifts will benefit the next generations of LGBTQ people, whose equality battles are yet to be known.

We have brought about great progress in the past, and staved off fierce challenges to that progress — and the future is expected to be no different. Thanks to our Legacy of Equality Society members, Equality Ohio will have the resources necessary to overcome the hurdles that lay ahead, uproot bigotry, and make Ohio a place where all people are welcome, affirmed, and flourishing.

This is a significant decision, and we appreciate you considering Equality Ohio while you are making it.
