The Coalition

Ohio Business Competes

Ohio Business Competes is a nonpartisan coalition of businesses committed to achieving LGBTQ‑inclusive nondiscrimination policies at the state level in order to attract the best talent, to increase Business‑to‑Business & Business‑to‑Consumer relationships, and to grow Ohio’s economy.

The coalition brings together Ohio corporations, small and medium sized enterprises, universities and law firms in support of LGBTQ‑inclusive nondiscrimination protections at the state level.

Why It Matters

Statewide protections are still missing

Ohio doesn’t have statewide, comprehensive laws prohibiting discrimination against LGBTQ+ people yet — Ohio is one of 29 states that leaves LGBTQ people out of laws that make discrimination illegal. See which local communities have enacted protections themselves on our Municipal Equality Map.
