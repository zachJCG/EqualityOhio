Eligibility

Who can be represented

To be represented by a lawyer from the Legal Clinic, your income must be within 300% of the federal poverty level. Not sure where you fall? Start the intake anyway — our team will walk through it with you, and can connect you to the Attorney Referral Network if the clinic isn’t the right fit.

Submitting an intake does not create an attorney-client relationship. Your information stays confidential within the clinic team.
