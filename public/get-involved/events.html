Evening for Equality · Dayton

The Roadmap Back to Equality

Thursday, August 27, 2026
5:30 PM arrival · 6:45 PM program
Private home in the Deweese Park neighborhood, Dayton — address provided upon RSVP.

Equality Ohio’s CEO and ED Dwayne Steward and the Obergefell & Arthur Society invite you for a conversation with Jim Obergefell, plaintiff in the historic marriage equality case, to learn how Ohioans can join the journey back to equality.
