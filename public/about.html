About Us

Who we are

Equality Ohio is Ohio’s statewide LGBTQ+ advocacy and legal aid organization — at city halls, the Statehouse, and in courtrooms across the state.

Vision

“An Ohio where justice, dignity, and self-determination belong to everyone.”

Mission

Equality Ohio drives innovative policy change locally and statewide, and delivers accessible legal support to advance lived and legal equality for LGBTQ+ Ohioans.

Our History

Rooted in advocacy since 2005

Equality Ohio was founded by 65 dedicated LGBT activists and allies from all corners of Ohio, after voters passed a constitutional amendment prohibiting same-sex marriage and civil unions. Two decades later, we’re still building the Ohio our community deserves.
